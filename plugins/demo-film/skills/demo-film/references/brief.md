# Film brief: <film id>

Half a page at the top of `plan.md`, one section per film when a project holds several. Ask the
user each question in plain words. The user confirms the brief before you write the timeline.

- **Audience.** Who watches, and what they already know. (Example: office managers at small
  building firms. They know their trade; they do not know this app.)
- **The one thing to show.** One sentence. If there are two, make two films.
- **The case.** Which page, which test record, which settings, and the figures that show on
  screen in those settings. Read the figures off the app, never from memory.
- **Data.** The test account and its fake data. Anything real that still shows and how it is
  hidden (`app.hideCss`, `app.textFixes`).
- **Permission.** Who owns the app, and who agreed to the film. This sentence goes into
  `app.permission`.
- **Must not appear.** Features, words, numbers or screens that would distract or mislead.
- **Length.** Default 20 to 70 s, silent.
- **Language.** The language of the app on screen and of the cards. Default English.
- **Ending.** The logo with one next step (brand), or the end line alone (no brand).
- **Open questions.** Anything unconfirmed that appears on screen.
