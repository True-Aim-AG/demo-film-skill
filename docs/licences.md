Remotion makes the films. Remotion has its own licence, separate from this repository.

| You are | Licence |
|---|---|
| An individual | Free |
| A for-profit company with up to 3 employees | Free |
| A non-profit organisation | Free |
| Evaluating Remotion, not yet in commercial use | Free |
| **A for-profit company with 4 or more employees** | **Company Licence, paid** |

A for-profit company with 4 or more employees must buy a Remotion Company Licence before it uses
Remotion to make videos. This includes internal and marketing videos. Buy it at
https://www.remotion.pro/license. The full terms are at https://www.remotion.dev/docs/license.

Remotion says that the licence changes slightly in version 5. The skill uses version 4. Read
the current terms before you buy.
